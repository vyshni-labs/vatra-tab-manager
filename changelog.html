Changelog

What changed in each version, newest first.

vatra on the Chrome Web Store

0.5.1 — October 2026

  • Fixed: with the panel docked on the left, the mode button sat on the hidden half of the resting strip and only showed on hover. It now sits on the side facing the page, in view, on either dock.

0.5.0 — October 2026

  • Three ways to keep vatra, one button: auto-hide, the pinned strip, or the pinned panel. Its icon is a picture of the layout it keeps. Click it for the next; hover it for a menu of all three, the current one marked, and pick any. New installs start with the strip pinned, and a short guide after install or update explains all three.
  • With the strip pinned, hovering a tab shows a wave: that tab's title, large, in the middle, with its two neighbours on each side getting smaller. Click any of them to switch.
  • Pinned tabs ring the first one in the strip. Hover and they open into a half-circle beside it.
  • The strip scrolls when more tabs are open than fit, and follows the tab you're on.
  • Small pinned icons show unread and call alerts as a coloured edge.
  • On the pinned strip, Tab onto a tab shows its wave; middle click on a card closes that tab.
  • New tab: a + at the end of the tab list.
  • The mode button and hide sit together at the top of the strip, in the same place in every view, with their own colours.
  • Fixed: a settings change made just as the panel opened could be undone a moment later.
  • Smoother, softer hover motion.
  • Clearer strip icons in the light theme. The colour theme's second accent outlines tabs and cards instead of colouring their titles.
  • No more tooltips over tabs.
  • Fixed: the panel and strip folded away when the pointer overshot into the screen edge.
  • Fixed: the bottom of the strip's icons was cut off, and its outline was missing an edge.
  • Fixed: with the strip pinned, hovering it threw it into the middle of the screen.
  • Fixed: a tab opened while the panel was showing could keep a grey globe instead of its site icon.
  • Fixed: all-white or all-black site icons (GitHub's, on a dark-mode system) vanished into their background; they are now shown in the opposite shade.
  • The panel starts faster — it no longer loads your tabs several times over.

0.4.8 — September 2026

  • A + at the foot of the strip opens a new tab, in the strip and the full panel alike.
  • Pin now holds one of three things: nothing, the whole panel, or just the strip. Click it to cycle.
  • With the strip pinned, hovering an entry slides out only that row — a tab, the search box, the view switch or the time window — so you can read it without opening the panel.

0.4.7 — August 2026

  • Slack tabs now show a dot when something is waiting. Slack puts no number in its tab title, only a marker, so a dot is what it can honestly say.

0.4.6 — August 2026

  • Reaching the docked screen edge opens the panel — no need to land on the thin strip.
  • Unread counts in a tab's title now show as a badge, so pinned tabs stop hiding the number you pinned them for.
  • A tab sitting in a video call gets a dot and a running clock.
  • A live strip above the pinned dock shows what's active right now. Settings → Signals turns it off.
  • Fixed: the panel sometimes ignored you when you reached for it.
  • Fixed: a console error after an update.

0.4.5 — August 2026

  • Fixed: hovering the strip often didn't open the panel.
  • Fixed: clicking a tab could do nothing right after an update.

0.4.4 — August 2026

  • Fixed: an error report after an update.

0.4.3 — August 2026

  • Your hottest tabs stay hottest after a restart.
  • The panel stops opening when you only sweep past it.
  • Closed lists tabs you actually closed.
  • Reopening a window full of tabs is lighter.

0.4.2 — August 2026

  • Clearer name and description on the Chrome Web Store.

0.4.1 — August 2026

  • Better search: closest match first, several words in any order, and typos still land.
  • Search is no longer reshuffled by the view, sort or time filter while you type.
  • The panel's controls stay readable over busy pages.
  • Faster typing with hundreds of tabs open.
  • Rate us link in Settings.
  • Fixed: the buttons down the left strip weren't aligned.

0.4.0 — August 2026

First public release.

  • The open-hearth mark and one warm scale carrying every heat and age signal.
  • Closed view — recently closed pages, one click to rekindle.
  • Alt+S opens the panel with the search box focused.
  • Chrome's native side panel as an alternative where the overlay can't appear.
  • Site icons come from Chrome's local cache, so browsing the panel contacts nothing.
  • Fixed: the panel disappearing on some web apps, ghosting while scrolling, and the shortcut going dead until restart.

Versions before 0.4.0 were development builds and were never released.
